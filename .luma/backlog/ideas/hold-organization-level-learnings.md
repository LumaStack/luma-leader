# Hold organization-level learnings

What has been discovered once and should not be rediscovered. `DECISIONS.md` is
the current form; whether learnings and decisions are the same shape is
unsettled.

## Notes

Migrated from `IDEAS.md` on 2026-08-20. **`created.at` is a day-level estimate**
— it is the date `IDEAS.md` was first committed, and the repository's history was
discarded before the migration, so nothing more precise is recoverable. The time
is a placeholder, not an observation.

**Kept whole, deliberately.** The entry holds a capability and an open format
question, and the two point at different repositories: holding learnings is built
here, while *whether learnings and decisions are the same shape* would be settled
in `luma-knowledge-format` or a catalog bundle. It was not split, because the
format question is not answerable until holding learnings has a shape of its own,
and half an idea filed elsewhere would be an orphan.

**The question is no longer hypothetical.** `DECISIONS.md` has grown past six
hundred lines and several entries in it are plainly learnings rather than
decisions — the naming criteria, *do not couple two open questions*, *removing a
file is not removing the disclosure*. The file is already doing both jobs.

# Templates for organization personas

Include templates for organization personas. Consider also supporting user
personas as this level, or maybe at the foreman level, I'm not sure yet.

## Notes

Migrated from `IDEAS.md` on 2026-08-21. `created.at` is a day-level estimate.

**Clarified at migration:** the idea covers **both internal roles and external
consumer roles**, where the latter applies. *Organization personas* was ambiguous
between the two, and the answer is that it is not a choice between them.

The open question in the entry stands and was deliberately not tidied away:
whether user-facing personas belong at this level or at foreman's. The two pull
different ways — internal roles are knowledge no single project has, while who
uses a given product is per-project — so it may resolve as both, at different
levels.

**Templates are not sensitive; filled-in personas are.** A completed set names
real people, real roles and real customers, and belongs in an organization's own
internal headquarters. The templates ship from here.

recorded well enough that the answer can be argued with later.

## Notes

Migrated from `IDEAS.md` on 2026-08-20. **`created.at` is a day-level estimate**
— it is the date `IDEAS.md` was first committed, and the repository's history was
discarded before the migration, so nothing more precise is recoverable. The time
is a placeholder, not an observation.

**`horizon: someday` is deliberate rather than defaulted.** This is aspirational,
and recording it explicitly says somebody judged it so.

**It may end up as a catalog bundle rather than something built here.** That does
not move the idea: this repository is the owner and overseer of such a bundle,
and is where the decision to publish one would be made.

The capability is not sensitive, but **its output probably is** — a prioritized
list of what an organization is about to build is competitive information. The
idea lives here; any organization's actual answer belongs in that organization's
own headquarters.
